# Privacy Policy for Grocify

**Last updated:** September 15, 2026

**Grocify** ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles and protects your information in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and global privacy regulations (including GDPR and CCPA).

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide Directly
- **Account Information:** When you register or update your profile, you may provide your name and email address.
- **Pantry, Inventory & Grocery Data:** Items you log in your pantry, fridge, or shopping lists (such as product name, category, quantity, expiry date, purchase date, location, and price).
- **Preferences:** User settings, such as theme preference (Dark/Light/System) and preferred currency (e.g., INR ₹).

### B. Technical & Device Information
- **Network State & Connectivity:** We check network connectivity (`android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`, `android.permission.INTERNET`) solely to synchronize your data with the cloud gateway or verify ML service availability.
- We do **not** collect your precise GPS location, contacts, call logs, camera images, microphone audio, or device advertising identifiers (GAID).

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to provide and improve Grocify's core features:
- **Pantry & Expiration Management:** Tracking food expiry dates and notifying you of items needing attention.
- **Smart Shopping Lists & Suggestions:** Auto-generating recommendations for out-of-stock or expiring items.
- **Budget & Expense Tracking:** Computing total household pantry spend and displaying localized expense breakdowns.
- **AI-Powered Recipe Generation:** Utilizing your existing ingredients to generate recipes (processed securely via encrypted API endpoints).

---

## 3. Data Storage & Security

- **Local Storage:** Grocify uses an on-device encrypted Room SQLite database and encrypted SharedPreferences to store your pantry items, shopping list, and session tokens locally on your device.
- **Data in Transit:** All communications between the app and backend/AI servers use HTTPS encryption in transit.
- **No Selling of Personal Data:** We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information or grocery data to third parties or data brokers.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services & AI Processing

- **Google Gemini API / AI Services:** If you utilize the AI Recipe Assistant or ML shelf-life predictions, ingredient names are securely sent to AI endpoints to return culinary guidance. No sensitive personal credentials or financial identifiers are sent to AI models.
- We do not integrate third-party advertising SDKs or invasive tracking ad networks.

---

## 5. Permissions Used & Why

Grocify adheres to the principle of least privilege. We only request install-time normal permissions:
- `android.permission.INTERNET`: Required to communicate with the cloud sync backend, verify recipe suggestions, and connect to remote services.
- `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: Required to detect whether your device is connected to Wi-Fi or cellular data for offline/online synchronization.

We do **not** require dangerous or runtime permissions.

---

- **Data Retention:** Your data is kept only for as long as you maintain your account or keep the application installed.
- **User Rights & Data Deletion:** You have the right to access, modify, or permanently delete your data. You can edit your profile directly in the app drawer. To request complete deletion of your account and associated cloud data, contact us at:

## 7. Children's Privacy

Grocify does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.
